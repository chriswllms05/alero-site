Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

The short version: Alero doesn't collect, sell or share your data. It has no account, no analytics and no tracking. Everything it records, transcribes and writes stays on your Mac.

What we collect

Nothing. We, the developer, don't receive any information about you or how you use Alero. There is no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting service and no advertising.

What stays on your Mac

  • Meeting recordings. When you choose to record, Alero saves the audio inside its own app folder on your Mac. By default recordings are deleted after 30 days; you can choose to delete them as soon as they're transcribed.
  • Transcripts and summaries. Alero transcribes with Apple's on-device speech recognition and drafts summaries and action items with Apple Intelligence, which runs on your Mac. The resulting meeting note is saved as a Markdown file in the folder you chose.
  • Your notes folder. Alero reads the folder of Markdown notes you pick, and edits it only when you act: checking off a task, adding one, moving one, or accepting an action item.
  • Settings. Your preferences, and which action items you accepted or dismissed, are stored on your Mac.

Permissions Alero asks for

  • Microphone: to record your side of a meeting you choose to record.
  • System audio: to record the other people on the call, for meetings you choose to record.
  • Calendars: to show today's meetings and their join links. Alero never changes your calendars.
  • Camera: for the optional mirror that shows you on camera before a call. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Automation (Spotify and Music): to show what's playing and to play, pause and skip when you ask.
  • A folder you choose: your Markdown notes.

You can change any of these in System Settings → Privacy & Security at any time.

When Alero uses the internet

  • Album art. To show artwork for the song that's playing, Alero sends the song and artist name to Apple's iTunes Search service, or the track link to Spotify. Apple's and Spotify's own privacy policies apply to those requests.
  • Purchases. The free trial, purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't see your payment details.
  • Apple system downloads. The first time you transcribe, macOS may download its on-device speech model.

Recordings, transcripts, summaries and your notes are never uploaded.

Recording other people

Laws about recording conversations vary by place, and some require everyone's consent. Alero reminds you to let participants know before you record, and never starts recording without your click (unless you turn on automatic recording yourself). You're responsible for following the laws that apply to you.

Children

Alero is a work tool and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Because Alero doesn't collect data, any change can only affect what happens on your Mac.
