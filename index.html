Alero

Meeting notes and your workday, in your Mac's notch.

Hover the notch to see today's meetings, join a call, and record it. Alero transcribes and summarizes the meeting on your Mac with Apple Intelligence, then turns what was decided into action items you can send to the right project.

Your projects and tasks come from a folder of plain Markdown notes you already keep. Check things off, add tasks, and move them between projects without leaving what you're doing.

Private by design. No account, no cloud, no tracking. Recordings, transcripts and summaries never leave your Mac.

Available on the Mac App Store. Requires macOS 26.
