Support

Help with Alero. Can't find your answer? Open a support request.

Getting started

Where is Alero?

Alero lives in your Mac's notch (or the top center of the screen on Macs without one). Hover there to open the panel. Its menu bar icon has Settings and Quit.

Which folder should I choose?

Any folder of Markdown notes, such as an Obsidian vault. Alero shows each project note's steps and tasks. Don't have one? Choose Start fresh during setup and Alero creates a folder with a sample project.

Meetings

How do I record a meeting?

Open the Meetings tab and click Record on the meeting, or choose Start Recording from the menu bar icon. When a call app starts using your microphone, Alero can also offer to record. It never records without your click unless you turn on automatic recording in Settings.

When does recording stop?

When you click Stop, when the call app quits, or after a few minutes of silence once the meeting's scheduled end has passed. A meeting that runs long keeps recording.

Why didn't I get a summary?

Summaries need a Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on (System Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri). Without it, Alero still saves the full transcript.

Where do my meeting notes go?

Into the meetings folder inside the folder you chose, as Markdown files. Action items appear in the Meetings tab, where you can send each one to a project or dismiss it.

Purchases

How does the trial work?

Every feature is free for 14 days. After that, Alero becomes read-only until you buy it once or subscribe. Your notes are never locked; they're plain files in your folder.

Restore a purchase or manage a subscription

In Alero's Settings, choose Restore Purchases. To change or cancel a subscription, open the App Store → your account → Subscriptions.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Alero has no account and collects no data. Recordings, transcripts and notes stay on your Mac. Read the privacy policy.
